# AIcookies — Manifesto

> **A cookie is the user's voice in the site.
> Let it speak well.**

---

## Today

Thirty years ago, cookies remembered your session.
Twenty years ago, they followed you.
Ten years ago, they profiled you.
Today they listen, watch, anticipate.

Every app generates more. Every banner asks for more.
Did you ever ask for any of that? No.

**The cookie never worked for you. It worked for the site that wrote it.**

---

## The shift

**AIcookies is not a cookie.** It's the architecture that reverses who works for whom on the web.

Four pieces, one doctrine:

| Piece | What it is | Where it lives |
|---|---|---|
| **The doctrine** | The seven principles | In this document |
| **The spec** | The open protocol between site and agent | In `SPEC.md` |
| **The Custodian** | The body · administers the cookies that already exist | In the browser · `CUSTODIAN.md` |
| **Dian** | The mind · reasons, decides, makes cookies intelligent | Inside the Custodian · `DIAN.md` |

The site no longer reads your cookies. It talks to the Custodian. When the Custodian needs to think, it asks Dian.

| Before | Now |
|---|---|
| The site asks for data | The site asks the Custodian |
| The user accepts or refuses | The Custodian decides what to answer |
| The site receives **data** | The site receives **answers** |

**The passive cookie remembered you. The Custodian represents you.**

---

## Seven principles

1. **The cookie serves the user, not the site.**
2. **The cookie reasons — it doesn't just store.**
3. **The cookie negotiates — it doesn't surrender.**
4. **Silence is a valid answer.**
5. **The cookie curates its own memory — deletes what no longer applies.**
6. **Local by default. Cloud is explicit exception.**
7. **The cookie is transparent — you can always ask what it knows and what it did.**

---

## The Custodian

The Custodian is the missing piece — the one that makes the rest real.

It does not replace the cookies you already have. It **administers** them. It lives in your browser, reads the thousands of cookies any site left there over the years, classifies them, decides banners on your behalf, prunes what no longer applies. It is the **older sibling** of your persistent web memory — the only agent with a full view and responsibility over it.

The function lives inside the name. **Cust·ODÍA·n** carries a *day* (Spanish *día*) inside: it brings light to a domain that for thirty years was dark to the user.

What it does:

- **Reads** cookies any site has already left in your browser.
- **Classifies** with LLM: essential · functional · tracker · ad · unknown.
- **Decides** banners for you, according to the principles you've stored.
- **Negotiates** generalized values when it can (age bracket instead of exact date).
- **Curates** memory — deletes what no longer applies, without asking the site that put it there.
- **Speaks the AIcookies spec** with sites that implement it.

Local-first. The doctrine is non-negotiable.

Inside the Custodian lives **Dian** — the reasoning module. Dian doesn't touch cookies, doesn't click buttons, doesn't speak to sites. Dian only thinks: classifies, decides, translates, negotiates. It is the only component of the project whose job is **to think** — and that is why it is the one that makes cookies intelligent.

Body specification in `CUSTODIAN.md` · mind specification in `DIAN.md`.

## What AIcookies IS

- A **doctrine** about who works for whom on the web.
- An **open protocol** between sites and agents (the spec).
- A **user's agent** (the Custodian) that embodies the doctrine and administers the cookies that already exist.

## What AIcookies IS NOT

- A better tracker.
- An AI working for the site.
- A wrapper for ad-tech.
- A closed product.

## Call

AIcookies is an **open standard**. The idea is free.
Implementation is built in community.
Doctrine certification is granted by the author.

- If you're an **engineer**: implement. The spec is open. The Custodian is the first reference implementation on the user side.
- If you're a **business**: respect it. Users notice.
- If you're a **user**: install the Custodian. It's your data — now with a representative.

## Lineage

The Custodian is the first user-side Kairo on the web. Dian, inside it, is the first Kairo whose only job is to think.
